Why is this surprising?

It's not about YOUR birthday. Matching one specific date needs a huge room (~253 people for 50/50). This is about ANY two people out of the whole room matching each other — and the number of possible pairs grows much faster than the number of people.

With just 23 people there are already 253 possible pairs (23×22÷2). Each pair only needs a tiny chance of matching, but 253 separate tiny chances add up fast — past 50% by n = 23, and past 99% by n = 70.

No real birthdays are used here — every "person" above is a computer-simulated random date. That's the same simulation logic (Monte Carlo) used throughout quantitative methods research to check math that's hard to work out by hand.

Nobody's real birthday is used in this demo — every person shown is randomly generated.
